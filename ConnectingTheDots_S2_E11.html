AI Platforms: How Stable Is the Leadership?

Last week’s question was whether arriving first decides leadership. This week’s question: how secure is this lead?

Onward — edition 11.

Since June, customer acquisition has plateaued. Among US businesses that Ramp tracks, Anthropic leads and OpenAI is a close second.

Spend shows a wider shift. Menlo, an Anthropic investor, has Anthropic and OpenAI taking most enterprise spend on AI models, with Anthropic well ahead. Google has fallen furthest.

The bigger question: who is holding the money?

Reuters reported two customers made up almost a quarter of Anthropic’s 2025 revenue. Anthropic’s filing shows none above 10% in the nine months to September 2026, with the top five at about 25%.

The majority of the spend still rests on a few businesses. Among businesses tracked by Ramp, about 1% account for about 80% of spending on OpenAI and Anthropic.

Skyworks learned what concentration costs. Apple alone was about two-thirds of its revenue. In February 2025, Skyworks warned its content in the next iPhone would fall by a fifth to a quarter, as analysts said Apple added a second supplier for one part. Apple stayed a customer.

Buyers are negotiating. Providers are cutting price, more businesses are switching than ever — AI is now a buyer’s market.

Winning the big buyers earns leadership. Spreading the revenue keeps it.
