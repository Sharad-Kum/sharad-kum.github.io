AI Platforms: Who Is Deciding the Leadership?

Not four years ago, “AI platform” meant one name.

Onward — edition 9.

ChatGPT opened to the public in November 2022. Google’s Bard followed in March 2023 and Claude in July. Bard is now Gemini.

Ramp sees what about 70,000 US businesses pay for. In March 2023, more than four in five of those paying for AI were paying OpenAI. Here is how each vendor’s reach has moved since, quarter by quarter.

Three phases. In 2023, OpenAI had the field almost to itself. Through 2024 and 2025, Anthropic built slowly. In early 2026 it nearly doubled in three months and passed OpenAI. Since June, both have flattened.

The large companies appear to have moved first. a16z’s January 2026 survey of Global 2000 firms found Anthropic in production up 25 points since May 2025, with OpenAI still first.

Menlo, an Anthropic investor, had Anthropic ahead in enterprise use by mid-2025.¹ a16z invests in OpenAI.

Worldwide, on consumer web visits, ChatGPT still leads. That is Similarweb.

Same market, read at different moments by people counting different things.
