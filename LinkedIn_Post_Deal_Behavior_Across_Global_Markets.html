Deal Behavior Across Global Markets

Over the last 2 months, I've spoken with decision-makers across ANZ, ASEAN, UK, Europe, and the Americas to understand how deal behavior and purchasing mindset in software services is actually shifting.

This isn't a reaction, it's a recalibration of how enterprises plan to buy technology solutions. The trigger may vary, the direction doesn't.

ANZ
Impact: Low–Moderate · Demand signal: Stable, cautious
Deal behavior: Vendor consolidation · Outlook: Defensive growth
ASEAN
Impact: Moderate · Demand signal: Slowing
Deal behavior: Delayed decisions · Outlook: Cost + outsourcing
UK
Impact: High · Demand signal: Weakening
Deal behavior: Renegotiation · Outlook: Cost-cutting mode
Europe
Impact: Moderate to High · Demand signal: Stagnant
Deal behavior: Phased deals · Outlook: Efficiency-driven
United States
Impact: Low · Demand signal: Strong
Deal behavior: ROI-focused · Outlook: AI-led growth
LatAm
Impact: Moderate · Demand signal: Soft
Deal behavior: Conservative · Outlook: Currency-sensitive

Decisions are slowing, deals are getting smaller and increasingly phased, and the pivot to outcomes over effort is now explicit. Transformation is being questioned, efficiency is being funded, and value is clearly overriding effort — at the same time, the ask for acceleration without compromising cost, quality, and stability is now non-negotiable.

Just a point of view at the intersection of Delivery, Technology and Global Markets.
