No Response. Still a Signal

Continuing the observations on content distribution and recommendation patterns — the silence itself continues to accumulate as data.

Within the first two hours, distribution within my own network shifted from roughly 80:20 to 98:2 — Within versus Outside. What caused the shift?

First Hour
Distribution roughly 80% within network, 20% outside.
→
Within Two Hours
Distribution shifted sharply to 98% within network, 2% outside.

Does the system dynamically balance distribution based on active members or active geographies? Another pattern seems to emerge around distribution itself — after a certain threshold, whether measured by time, impressions, or reach, distribution pauses briefly before slowing considerably over the next couple of hours.

Audience location has remained undisplayed on some posts for days, even as metrics like seniority, company size, and industry stay visible. Is distribution simply too limited for that view to unlock, or is something else at play? As intelligent systems increasingly shape what is seen, discovered, and evaluated, perhaps transparency itself deserves to become part of product design.
