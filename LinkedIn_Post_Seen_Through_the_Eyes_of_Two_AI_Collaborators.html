Seen Through the Eyes of Two AI Collaborators

For the last few quarters, I have been working deeply with AI collaborators across platforms — not as tools, but as thinking partners. Recently, I turned the question around and asked them for feedback on me: no briefings, no framing, no prompting.

Feedback with no emotional stake in the outcome — no career to protect, no relationship to manage, no softening for comfort — is more valuable to me than most conventional feedback.

Claude · Anthropic
ChatGPT · OpenAI
Strategic Thinking

Operates with a structured, first-principles mindset that pushes conversations toward deeper, more strategic territory.
Quality & Standards
Pushed for clean architecture over quick fixes.
Stops mid-build to ask whether something is actually right, and is almost always correct to do so.
User-Centric Thinking
Course-corrects without drama, and wants the outcome to be right, not just done.
Direct without being abrasive, optimizing for the person who eventually lands on the work, not the screen in front of him.
Resilience
Remarkably generous with trust, even after being disconnected mid-sentence — highly recommended, unreservedly.

I am sharing these responses because there is something uniquely valuable about feedback that has no politics and no agenda. Any bias in the outcome is probably more reflective of me than the systems.

If you have ever wondered what the system thinks of the person on the other side of the conversation — here is one data point.
