The Business Leader Who Knows Technology

For years we called them technology leaders who'd earned a seat at the business table. That framing always seemed slightly wrong.

The person closest to the roadmap is now expected to be the person closest to the P&L.

TSMC · Taiwan
Leadership grounded in electrical engineering now holds fab economics, capital allocation, and geopolitics in the same conversation
Revenue: ~$34B (2018) → $122.4B (2025)
Wix · Israel
Technically founded leadership scaled a website builder into positioning, category ownership, and a public market
Revenue: $1.39B → $2B; operating cash flow doubled
Nokia · Finland
A genuine turnaround: the business restructured around infrastructure and cloud/AI networking
Operating margin: 8.3% → 19.1%
Technology Leader
Earns a seat at the table.

Business Leader
Whose native language is technology.

Ownership of commercial outcomes, market positioning, and competitive strategy has become part of the job itself, not an addition to it. Ask what a technology leader is measured on today, and the answer increasingly includes revenue impact, customer acquisition cost, and how a platform decision shapes go-to-market timing. None of these sit inside technology alone.

Markets, solutions, and delivery aren't three functions converging on one leader. They're becoming the definition of the role.
