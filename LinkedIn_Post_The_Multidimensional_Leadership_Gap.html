The Multidimensional Leadership Gap

As expectations from leaders expand beyond functional expertise to encompass market awareness, integrated solution thinking, and execution credibility — is the ecosystem keeping pace?

Are we building, hiring, developing, and rewarding leaders for these broader expectations — or are many of our systems still optimized for functional specialization?

Built For
Depth within a single function — the long-standing measure of leadership readiness.
→
Now Expected
Market awareness, integrated solution thinking, and execution credibility, beyond functional expertise.

Many leadership frameworks, career paths, succession plans, and reward structures were designed for a world where functional depth was the primary measure. Today's opportunities and challenges rarely fit neatly within a single discipline.

Is the demand for multidimensional leaders growing faster than the ability to fulfill them — a temporary transition, or the emergence of a persistent leadership gap? Have our talent models, role definitions, hiring frameworks, and evaluation mechanisms evolved enough to recognize and value such leaders when they do emerge?
