GTM, Solution and Delivery: The Myth of Three Silos

Market awareness needs to continually improve. Solution needs to solve problems beyond technology. Delivery needs to move beyond the Statement of Work. Outcomes need to remain relevant to consumers and markets.

Data, Process, Systems, Feedback and Decisions have existed for decades — the differentiator is the ability to connect them intelligently.

This pattern is already visible across the organizations leading their markets
ServiceNow · United States
Its acquisition of Logik.ai in 2025 was framed as the ability to “sell, fulfill, and service on a single platform”
Opportunity, quoting, order, and fulfillment joined end-to-end, instead of handed off across separate systems
Telstra International · Australia
A unified network inventory platform lets sales and engineering move from design straight to deployment
Provisioning once taking 60–70 days now compressed to about a week
Schneider Electric · France
Its EcoSet Config platform unifies design, validation and quotation in one electrical project cycle
Connected through mySchneider, from ordering to support

Organizations translating this intelligence into market-aligned execution, resulting in measurable impacts, are the ones remaining ahead on the relevance curve.

AI-enabled capabilities are one such wave today; tomorrow there may well be another.
